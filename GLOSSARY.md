# Lumo

Local reading and page translation.

## Language

**Reading view**:
A book’s presentation choices and remembered reading and scroll positions. This is distinct from the arrangement of the original and translation panes.

**Screen page**:
The visible range of an EPUB book determined by the viewport and reading settings; one section can contain many screen pages.

**Neighbouring context**:
Excerpts beside the page being translated, supplied for continuity but not translated or saved.

**Signed update**:
An offered newer Lumo release whose downloaded package is verified before installation.
